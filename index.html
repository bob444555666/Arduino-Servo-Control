Before you start

Servo type matters: continuous-rotation servos spin continuously and support speed/direction control. A standard positional servo only moves to an angle; it cannot spin continuously just by changing this code.

For a powerful or fast servo, use a separate regulated 5V supply rated for its current. Connect the supply ground to Arduino GND. Do not draw high servo current from the Mega's 5V pin. Secure the servo and anything attached before testing.

Upload arduino/servo_controller.ino using the Arduino IDE first. Close the IDE Serial Monitor before connecting this page.
